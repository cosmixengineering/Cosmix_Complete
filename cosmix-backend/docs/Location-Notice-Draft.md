# Location notice — user-approved wording, 26 October 2026

Cosmix uses your device location when you request attendance check-in or checkout to confirm that you are at your assigned workplace. Check-in also requires camera-based face and liveness verification. The app explains failures and does not confirm attendance unless the backend accepts the required evidence.

This attendance flow does not perform background location tracking. Location coordinates are retained for 180 days, after which scheduled cleanup removes them from attendance records and retained capture evidence. Attendance dates, check-in/out times, approved corrections and daily summaries remain available under the company's separate employment-record retention policy.

Access is restricted to authorized company personnel and the services needed to operate attendance. If you decline or cannot provide the required evidence, contact HR to arrange review through the company's approved correction process. A missed checkout is sent for HR review and is not, by itself, an automatic salary deduction.

Select Accept only after reviewing this notice. A revised published notice requires fresh acceptance. Contact company HR for questions about attendance data, corrections or access requests.
