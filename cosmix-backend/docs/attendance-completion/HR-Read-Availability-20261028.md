## Problem and correction

Opening an employee calendar could call the strict `LatestAsync` path for a locked date without a retained summary. It raised `locked_attendance_summary_unavailable`, aborted the entire calendar, and could pause Visual Studio while the authorization transaction remained open.

The scoped HR daily and calendar GET endpoints now use `AttendanceDayReadResponse`. A missing frozen summary is represented on that date with `factualStatus: "unavailable"`, `isLocked: true`, and `unavailableReason: "locked_attendance_summary_unavailable"`. Facts, roster, policy, exception count, timezone and saved revision are null. Available dates keep their actual saved evidence. A missing record is never treated as zero time, absence, or zero pay.

The strict repository, calculation and mobile/self contracts remain unchanged. Valid frozen records still require matching employee/date and fingerprint. Corrupt evidence, storage failures and unauthorized reads are not converted into missing coverage. No lock is reopened and no historical record is repaired or generated by this change.
