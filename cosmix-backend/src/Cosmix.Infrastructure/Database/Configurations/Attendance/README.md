# Attendance persistence mappings

The initial mappings cover sites, geofence versions, shifts, shift versions, employee shift
assignments and raw punch evidence in the `attendance` schema. Domain IDs are generated by
constructors. SQL constraints cover valid coordinates, explicit version starts, enums,
schedule duration/minutes, effective assignment intervals and UTC punch timestamps.

Relationships restrict deletion. Editable roots carry rowversion. Composite foreign keys
bind a geofence to its site and an HR assignment to its employee. Per-employee idempotency
keys are case-sensitive and unique; a request fingerprint alone is not a retry workflow.
Raw punches have no unique employee/day constraint. No business data or policy defaults
are seeded. The context owns tracked append-only guards for raw events and versions.

Migrations are centrally coordinated under `Database/Migrations`. Build/model verification
does not replace SQL Server constraint and transaction testing or complete business services.
