## Authority And Configurability

- Company profile and Head Office configuration reads/history/writes require current SuperAdmin authority and an explicit supported All-scope permission. No automatic ordinary Admin Company grant was introduced.
- Attendance and leave policy configuration, validation/publication and assignment changes require fixed Admin/SuperAdmin authority and a matching permission assignment. Ordinary scoped HR applicability reads, correction decisions, overtime decisions and geofence permissions are preserved.
- Overtime eligibility policy mutations require fixed Admin/SuperAdmin authority plus their matching current/original scope. Eligibility reads remain usable under their existing permissions. No role name alone grants authority.
- Leave settings v2 accepts explicit quotas, service threshold, standard-day minutes, eligible categories, holiday treatment, paid kinds and annual start month/day. The frontend publishes v2 explicitly and reads retained v1 or v2. There are no new hidden entitlement defaults.
- Retained v1 policies retain the original SRS interpretation and JSON. Existing utilization/release denominators remain historical. Changing a year boundary cannot hide recorded or cancelled usage; unsupported transition reconciliation remains blocked for that candidate. A conflicting candidate no longer hides another usable policy.
- Existing approved OT valuation is preserved, not repriced. A broader versioned OT/meal/vehicle/loan configuration and the remaining operational engines are separate unfinished work; this report does not claim every SRS parameter is now configurable.
- The historical `DirectHr` policy enum is retained for compatibility. It describes stored publication provenance, not permission for a nonfixed HR role to configure policy today.
