The responsible operator reviews the runner, the generated `Current12-to-FrozenA23.sql`, and its frozen manifest before invocation. Supply the SQL and manifest paths with their reviewed SHA256 hashes. The manifest must contain exactly 23 ordered records with `MigrationId` and the actual target-model `ProductVersion`; current SQL history must equal its first 12 records. The runner accepts only the selected existing operator-owned `CosmixUat_d893653346` instance and `Cosmix_Testing` catalog, the known backup family, 5 existing accounts, 14 employees, and all 3 email identities read privately from `Test-Accounts.json`. Fixture passwords are never used or printed.

Bounds are 5 seconds to connect, 180 seconds per SQL command, and a 20 minute command-dispatch window; a command already dispatched can run until its own timeout. Inventory permits at most 100,000 rows per table and 200,000 rows total, with one data/log pair totaling at most 10 GiB and at most 2,000 reviewed SQL batches. A bound failure preserves the attempt for review.

All new artifacts are retained under a unique owned directory inside `%LOCALAPPDATA%/Cosmix/PartAContinuation/Database20261013`, whose private owner/ACL and non-linked ancestry must already pass. The runner verifies the existing B05 backup's private provenance, actual SHA256 and header without restoring it. Private evidence records table names, source column names and digests, without retaining account passwords or raw business rows. The runner refuses any changed retained fact, missing table, altered history, added original-table row, or seeded new business table; it does not relax such a failure automatically.

The reviewed idempotent pending range executes **only through the clone connection**, split at actual standalone `GO` lines outside comments and quoted constructs. SQLCMD directives, repeat counts, target switching, backup/restore statements, deletes, truncation and retained table/column drops are refused. No original application is authorized by this result; current Part B24 still requires its own reviewed migration and coordinated handoff.
