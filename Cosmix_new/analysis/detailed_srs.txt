Cosmix ERP FSM Detailed Module Specification
Complete module contracts workflows validation permissions and acceptance
This version expands the v0.3 summary into a detailed requirements reference for management, domain teams and beginner developers. It defines the information each module handles, the actions it permits, the route through approval and execution, the handling of invalid requests and the evidence required to accept the implementation.
Main conclusion
Business records have one authoritative owner; web/mobile panels reuse those records under server-side permissions. Required approval, evidence, effective dates and valid state transitions must be checked before a consequential action. Known source values are preserved, and unresolved values remain management decisions rather than developer guesses.
How to use the document
Read the common contracts first, then the relevant module specification. Each module identifies fields and validation categories, trigger/actions, ordered workflow, ownership, status/error rules and acceptance cases. The HR annex reproduces the supplied detailed field/rule/decision tables. The final baseline catalogue preserves the 62 AI-free source sections for detailed scope lookup.
Evidence versus design proposals
Source-required fields and rules retain their supplied meaning. Additional record labels, contract fields, state labels, retry/version controls and ownership splits in this expansion are proposed design for approval unless explicitly supplied by HR v1.2. They are not verified SQL tables, existing API URLs, implemented features or a fixed legal/company policy. Requiredness, lengths, precision, limits, SLA values and authorization thresholds not given by the sources must be signed off.

End to end business routes and state gates
The three customer-order, complaint and employee journeys are independent entry routes. A customer complaint is not forced through a new BOQ/quotation. Manufacturing, QA and goods delivery remain conditional. Billing occurs at approved transaction timing, not automatically after every complaint closes.
Intake connectors payloads and routing requirements
Canonical record and processing
Retain intake ID, source/account/provider event key, thread, original message/files, sender/contact, received and source times, reviewed customer/site link, category/urgency, required-data result, assigned department/user, current state, business reference and correlation/history. Quarantined files and invalid-origin events cannot enter ordinary business execution. Uncertain customer match/category goes to review.
Route enquiry or customer PO to Sales; complaint to Operations; supplier response to Procurement; internal support to Admin. A receiving owner or explicit fallback queue must retain unresolved work. Unknown provider outcome is reconciled before repeating consequential sends. Redelivery/out-of-order callbacks must not duplicate or regress the business action.

Human Resources ownership and review boundary
Employee identity, employment lifecycle, attendance and employee policies.
Source: Original sections 42-44 and 48; detailed HR SRS v1.2 sections 2-19.
Inputs: approved employee data, dated policies, assignments, punch/report evidence and employee requests. Outputs: employee eligibility, approved attendance/claims and locked payroll input.
HR owns attendance and employee-policy decisions. Accounts consumes verified payroll input; it does not rewrite raw punches. Operations owns job assignment, not employee medical data. Face verification belongs only to Check In.
Domain decisions
Confirm approval chain, face provider, GPS accuracy/radius, policy conflicts, rates, retention and payroll boundary from the HR decision register.
HR01 Employee master and lifecycle
Owner: Human Resources. Trigger: New hire or employment change.
Purpose and result: Draft, required identity/employment fields, approval, activation, effective assignments and offboarding. Output: Employee record and lifecycle decision.
Field groups and validation
Actions and ordered workflow
Allowed actions: Save permitted draft/source; validate/submit; required approval; allowed execute/record action; status/history/report; revision or adjustment replaces silent edits.
Workflow: 1. Save Draft; 2. validate required fields/uniqueness; 3. submit; 4. authorized approve; 5. activate or effective-date change.
States controls and failure paths
Draft > Pending Approval > Active; dated Suspended, Resigned, Terminated or Retired; referenced records archive instead of deletion.
Draft never grants attendance access. Server allocates unique employee number.
Dated salary/manager/site/shift/status history and session revocation must follow the approved lifecycle.
Acceptance cases
HR01-A01: authorized new hire or employment change follows the listed workflow and returns employee record and lifecycle decision.
HR01-A02: invalid employee identity and number or missing data required for this action returns a clear error/review; an explicitly allowed incomplete Draft remains inactive. HR01-A03: changed user/record scope cannot expose or alter unauthorized data.
HR01-A04: identical retry creates no second consequential effect; stale/conflicting edit or illegal transition is rejected. Failure retains a visible pending/review/error result rather than false success.
Dependencies and source: Original sections 42-44 and 48; detailed HR SRS v1.2 sections 2-19.; owner rules, referenced upstream records, shared authorization/approval/files/history/reporting and the applicable downstream outcome. All unspecified rates/limits/requiredness need sign-off.

HR04 Face and attendance
Owner: Human Resources. Trigger: Employee starts or ends duty.

1 System Purpose and Scope
The solution will provide one controlled HR and attendance platform for office employees, site staff, supervisors, HR, Accounts and management. Employees will use the mobile application for check-in, check-out and self-service. Authorized office users will use the React web application for employee administration, shifts, attendance review, policy configuration, approvals, reports and payroll handoff.
1.1 Primary Objectives
Maintain a reliable employee master with personal, employment, contact, document, assignment and compensation information.
Capture attendance through the mobile application with face verification at Check In and authorized correction without losing original evidence.
Apply office and site policies consistently using versioned rules with effective dates.
Give each employee secure access to their own attendance, salary summary, deductions, overtime, leave, advance and loan information.
Provide traceable approvals and audit history for corrections, overtime, leave, allowances and policy exceptions.
Generate verified attendance outputs for Accounts and payroll without allowing Accounts to rewrite original punches.
1.2 Included Scope
Authentication, session management, password recovery and role-based access control.
Employee master, onboarding, employment history, documents, certifications, emergency details and status lifecycle.
Departments, designations, reporting lines, branches, sites, staff categories, shifts, rosters and holidays.
Mobile check-in/check-out, face verification at Check In, geolocation evidence, server timestamps and attendance exceptions.
In-app site arrival/departure reporting and employee-specific authorized, controlled and restricted operational zones.
Office and site attendance rules, overtime, meal allowance eligibility, travel day treatment and attendance corrections.
Leave requests, balances, evidence, approval and attendance integration.
Employee self-service web/mobile views and supervisor-limited views.
Fuel, motorcycle tuning and vehicle custody policy records where they affect employees and payroll claims.
Reports, exports, notifications, audit logs and payroll-ready attendance summaries.
1.3 Out of Scope for the First Production Release
Automatic salary disbursement to a bank. The first release may generate an approved payroll handoff or bank advice file only.
Medical diagnosis or AI-based disciplinary decisions.
Unrestricted live tracking outside an authorized attendance or assigned field-work event.
Attendance-machine, time-clock or other attendance-hardware integration. The mobile application is the production attendance channel.
Final payroll accounting ledger if Accounts chooses to retain it in a separate finance module.

3 Technology and Architecture
3.1 Logical Request Flow
1. React or React Native sends an authenticated HTTPS request to the ASP.NET Core API.
2. The API validates the access token, role, employee/site scope and request schema.
3. The application service loads effective policy, shift, roster and assignment data for the event date.
4. The domain service calculates or validates the result and records source evidence, result and audit metadata in one transaction.
5. Notifications and noncritical processing run through background jobs after the transaction commits.

Development, Test/UAT and Production must use separate databases, storage, secrets and API endpoints.
Production secrets must be stored in a secure secret manager or protected server configuration, never in React or React Native source code.
Demo/mock data must never be mixed with production employee records.

7 Attendance Processing
Attendance must preserve evidence in two layers. Raw Punch Events store every received check-in/out event unchanged. Daily Attendance Summaries derive work time, status, late minutes, overtime and policy effects. A correction changes the interpreted summary or adds an authorized adjustment; it must not delete the original punch.
7.1 Attendance Sources
7.2 Mobile Check In and Check Out Flow
1. Employee signs in and the app loads the current assignment, shift and attendance state from the API.
2. For Check In, the app starts a live face challenge and requests location permission for the attendance event. Check Out does not require face verification.
3. The app confirms liveness and a one-to-one match against the signed-in employee's active face enrollment, then obtains a short-lived verification result/token.
4. The app captures coordinates, accuracy, device time and device identifier, then sends the event and required verification result over HTTPS.
5. The server uses its own receipt time as the authoritative time and validates assignment, duplicate state, allowed time window and configurable geofence.
6. The server stores the raw event, returns accepted, accepted with exception, queued for review or rejected with a clear reason.
7. The app displays the server-confirmed time, location/site and attendance status. A locally displayed success without server confirmation is not a valid punch.
7.2.1 Check In Face Verification
7.3 Attendance Correction
1. Employee or authorized supervisor submits the correction with requested time/status, reason and evidence.
2. HR or the configured authority reviews source punches, assignment, shift and evidence.
3. Approval creates a correction record and recalculates the daily summary using the applicable policy version.
4. Rejection records the reviewer and reason and notifies the requester.
5. If the payroll period is locked, the correction requires reopening or an adjustment in the next payroll period according to management policy.

CORE01 Identity and scope | Generate stable IDs in backend. Resolve actor/customer/employee/team/site/store permissions from trusted context. A supplied ID is not authority. Reject out-of-scope read/write/export.
CORE02 Field validity | Validate required source fields, type, controlled choices, referenced records and allowed conditional fields. Return errors per field. Never use UI validation as the only check.
CORE03 Quantities and amounts | Validate numeric values and units. Required order/issue quantities must be positive; permitted signed correction deltas require explicit authority. Decimal precision, rounding, negative-credit and valuation rules need owner sign-off.
CORE04 Date and rules | Use valid intervals and the policy/assignment/rate applicable on the business date. HR server time/Pakistan timezone is authoritative; device time is retained as evidence.
CORE05 Approval and status | Backend validates current state, reviewer authority/value limit, explicit/time-bound delegation and required evidence. Requester cannot self-approve HR requests. Reject invalid transition or unauthorized Approved/Closed flags.
CORE06 Retry and concurrent edit | Consequential create/approval/punch/movement/post actions have stable retry protection. Return prior result for identical retry; conflicting payload/version must not silently overwrite newer work.
CORE07 Atomic record changes | Movement, posting and related consequential records succeed together or roll back together. External sends are queued/tracked; database success does not imply provider delivery.
CORE08 Files and privacy | Validate extension, MIME/content signature, size policy and malware scan before allowed download/use. Private metadata/files require server permission. Do not place secrets/salary/medical evidence in public URLs or lockscreens.
CORE09 Original and history | Retain original query, raw punch, issued document and approved policy/calculation basis. Correction/revision/adjustment stores before/after values, actor, reason, time and source links.
CORE10 Reports and export | Apply row and column scope, pagination/filter/sort, real generation and authorized download. Export is auditable and cannot expose data hidden from the requesting role.
CORE11 Error and notification | Use stable error categories, human-readable reason, permitted field details and correlation/reference ID. Failed/pending/review outcomes are visible. Safe notification links back to the source decision.
CORE12 Retention and release | Archival/offboarding and deletion follow approved retention, dependency and private-file rules. A completed specification is not release acceptance; UAT and signed decisions are required.
