3. SYSTEM OBJECTIVE
The HMS platform shall operate as an integrated:
ERP + CRM + Estimation + Engineering + Procurement + Inventory/MM + Manufacturing + Project Management + Field Service Management + HR + Finance + Communication + AI Automation Platform.
The objective is to provide one controlled business flow from the first customer inquiry through costing, quotation, procurement, project execution, delivery, service, accounting, and after-sales activities.
