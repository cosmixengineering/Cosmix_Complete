## 2. Concrete Findings

The 21 destinations above have no page routes; selecting a visible link falls through to the `/` redirect. ### F02 [P1] New review APIs authenticate users but do not authorize business decisions

Intake, engineering, customer PO, QA and unit-conversion groups use bare `RequireAuthorization()` without scoped permission or feature checks. Engineering accepts `ReviewedBy` from the request and QA accepts `InspectedBy`; neither derives the decision actor from current-user context. A signed-in caller can attempt these mutations without a domain-approver policy, including when the module is disabled, and can supply another reviewer's name. Evidence: `src/NOIR.Web/Endpoints/IntakeEndpoints.cs:9`, `src/NOIR.Web/Endpoints/EngineeringEndpoints.cs:9`, `src/NOIR.Web/Endpoints/EngineeringEndpoints.cs:48`, `src/NOIR.Web/Endpoints/EngineeringEndpoints.cs:64`, `src/NOIR.Web/Endpoints/CustomerPoEndpoints.cs:10`, `src/NOIR.Web/Endpoints/QaEndpoints.cs:7`, `src/NOIR.Web/Endpoints/QaEndpoints.cs:59`, `src/NOIR.Web/Endpoints/UnitConversionEndpoints.cs:9`.

BOQ demonstrates the stronger existing pattern: a module requirement, named approve policy and server-supplied user ID at `src/NOIR.Web/Endpoints/BoqEndpoints.cs:27`, `src/NOIR.Web/Endpoints/BoqEndpoints.cs:162`, `src/NOIR.Web/Endpoints/BoqEndpoints.cs:166`. Generic tenant filters remain present, so this finding concerns missing authority within the selected tenant, not a demonstrated cross-tenant exploit. SRS: CORE01/CORE05 and trusted reviewer rules, `../analysis/detailed_srs.txt:3188`, `../analysis/detailed_srs.txt:3192`, `../analysis/detailed_srs.txt:3239`.

### F11 [P2] Reference validation, retries and conflict handling are incomplete

The direct intake/engineering/PO/QA handlers create records from request IDs/strings without loading and validating referenced customer/project/BOQ/department/user/source records. Request models have no field validators in these paths. PO creation permits a supplied amount without a positive-value guard in its factory; QA permits missing reference/evidence. Database length/unique indexes are not field-level business validation.
